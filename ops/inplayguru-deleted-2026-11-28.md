## Rules

**Super favorite conceded goal in first half** · Leagues: all
- Pre-match 1X2 odds of favourite ≤ 1.7
- Goals of underdog (1st half) ≥ 1

**GS Over 1.5 at 0-0** · Leagues: Betfair only
- Match minute ≤ 70; goals = 0
- Pre-match 1X2 odds of favourite ≤ 1.95
- Live odds, match goals Over 1.5: ≥ 1.5 and ≤ 2.19
- xG of favourite − xG of underdog ≥ 0.3
- Favourite's last 10 matches: 1.5+ match goals ≥ 75%, and 0.5+ second-half goals ≥ 75%

**GS Match Odds Fave** · Leagues: Betfair only
- Match minute ≤ 70; goals = 0
- Pre-match 1X2 odds of favourite ≤ 1.95
- Live 1X2 odds of favourite: ≥ 1.5 and ≤ 2.19
- xG of favourite − xG of underdog ≥ 0.3

**Favourite pressure 2nd half** · Leagues: Betfair only
- Match minute ≥ 50 and ≤ 70
- Favourite's passing accuracy ≥ 65%
- Pre-match 1X2 odds of favourite ≤ 2.00
- Momentum of favourite ≥ 63
- Goals of favourite − goals of underdog ≤ 0, and goals of underdog − goals of favourite ≤ 1
- Live odds, match goals over (next goal) ≥ 1.4

**Action-packed** · Leagues: Betfair only
- Momentum ≥ 97; match minute ≤ 77
- Live odds, match goals Over 0.5 ≥ 1.3
- xG in the past 15 minutes ≥ 0.38
- Momentum of either team ≥ 30, both 10 and 5 minutes ago
- Shots on target in the past 10 minutes ≥ 2

**Time to fight** · Leagues: Betfair only
- Goals of underdog ≥ goals of favourite + 1
- Momentum ≥ momentum of favourite + 39
- Live odds, match goals over (next goal) ≥ 1.4
- Match minute < 75; red cards of either team ≤ 0

**Underdog taking charge** · Leagues: Betfair only
- Goals of underdog ≥ goals of favourite + 1
- Momentum of underdog ≥ momentum of favourite + 33
- Match minute < 84
- Live 1X2 odds of underdog ≥ 1.28; live 1X2 odds of losing team ≥ 10
- Live odds, match goals over (next goal) ≥ 1.25
